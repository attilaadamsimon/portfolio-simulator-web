Method & data

Correlated monthly lognormal paths fitted to up to five years of adjusted closing prices. Buy and hold, dividends reinvested within each holding, no rebalancing or deposits. Currency movements are included. Options, fees and taxes are excluded. Holdings with insufficient history are automatically excluded and listed with a reason; their values are omitted from the starting portfolio. Historical returns are uncertain estimates, not forecasts.

GBM is the original default. Merton adds compensated shared jumps; Heston uses stochastic variance with four substeps per month; bootstrap resamples monthly return rows or circular blocks. Model comparison uses matching inputs and seeds, not identical shocks across different models. Stress scenarios are illustrative constant regimes. The drift control is an annual log-return rate, not a promised simple return.
